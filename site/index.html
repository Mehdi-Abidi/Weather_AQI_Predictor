Method

How it works

  1. Collect. Hourly pollutant estimates and daily weather for Karachi come from the free Open-Meteo APIs, which serve the CAMS global model.
  2. Describe. Each day becomes 41 numbers: the last seven days of PM2.5, rolling means, other pollutants, wind, humidity, rain, pressure and the time of year.
  3. Forecast. One Random Forest per horizon predicts the daily mean one, two and three days ahead.
  4. Check. The models are scored on a full year they never trained on, against a baseline that repeats today's value.

04 · Honest notes

What did not work

  • The 2024 version classified the AQI category with a random train and test split. It scored 79 percent, and so did a model that always answered "very poor", because 177 of 225 test rows had that label.
  • Predicting the category directly never beat persistence once the split was by time.
  • Tuned XGBoost and LightGBM did not beat the Random Forest on this amount of data.
  • Even with perfect knowledge of the next three days of weather, R² only reaches about 0.47. Three days out, much of the variation is not predictable from this data.

Caveats

Read this before trusting it

  • The values are model estimates for the grid cell around Karachi, about 40 km wide. They are not readings from a ground station.
  • The bands follow the OpenWeather PM2.5 scale: good below 10, fair below 25, moderate below 50, poor below 75, very poor from 75.
  • This is a student project and not a health advisory.
